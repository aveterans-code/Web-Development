Standard Operating Procedures

  1. Verify weather conditions (wind < 25 kts)
  2. Check battery level (minimum 90%)
  3. Confirm GPS lock (minimum 8 satellites)
  4. Test communication link (signal > 80%)
  5. Verify mission parameters loaded
  6. Clear launch area of personnel

Loss of Signal Protocol:
  • Drone automatically initiates RTB after 30 seconds
  • Climbs to safe altitude (500m)
  • Returns via direct path to launch point
  • Lands automatically if signal not restored
